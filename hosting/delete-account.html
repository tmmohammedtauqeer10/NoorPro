Delete Account and Data

Updated: October 1, 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Noor Pro and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Account > Delete account (also available in Settings > Support > Delete account).
  3. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete my account. For your security you may be asked to enter your password or sign in with Google again.

Your sign-in account is deleted immediately and the app signs you out.

Request deletion without the app

If you can no longer open the app, email noorpro.official@gmail.com from the address registered to your account with the subject Noor Pro account deletion request. We may ask you to verify ownership and will complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, display name, sign-in identifiers).
  • Your public profile, username, bio and profile photo.
  • Your posts and reels (text, photos, videos), comments, likes, saves, follows, and blocked-user list, where the app can remove them.
  • Your cloud settings backup and notification/feedback data linked to your account.
  • Messages you sent in chats and groups, and groups you own, where the app can remove them.

Reel video files that were transcoded for streaming and chat media that cannot be removed by the app are purged by us after your request, within 30 days.

What may remain

Reports you filed or that were filed against content, and moderation, security and legally required records, may be kept for a limited period where permitted by law (up to 12 months). Anonymised or aggregated data and standard server logs are not linked to you. Copies of messages already delivered to other people may remain on their devices. Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling Noor Pro.

Local data only

To remove local data immediately, open Android Settings > Apps > Noor Pro > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.
