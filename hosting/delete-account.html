Delete Account and Data

To remove local Noor Pro data immediately, open Android Settings > Apps > Noor Pro > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Request authenticated-account deletion

  1. Open Noor Pro and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Open Settings > Feedback.
  3. Send a request with the subject Noor Pro account deletion request.
  4. Include the email address associated with your Noor Pro account.

We may ask you to verify ownership. After verification, the Firebase Authentication account and associated user-identifiable Ummah reports will be deleted where applicable. Approved public posts are not currently created by users.

What remains

Aggregated, anonymized, legally required, or security-related records may be retained where permitted by law. Data stored only on your device cannot be removed remotely.
