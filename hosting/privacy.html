Privacy Policy

Effective: June 20, 2026

Noor Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Android app processes and why.

Information you provide

If you create an account or sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication credentials. Noor Pro never receives your Google password.

Location information

With your permission, Noor Pro uses device location to calculate prayer times, identify an approximate location label, and calculate Qibla direction. Location is stored locally for future calculations and may be sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time API to retrieve prayer and Hijri information.

App activity and local data

Bookmarks, prayer logs, Qaza counts, Noor Points, settings, downloaded reading data, and learning progress are primarily stored locally on your device. Backup files are created only when you explicitly request them.

Firebase and Ummah community

Firebase Authentication supports account sign-in. The Ummah community feed lets signed-in users publish posts (text, captions, and uploaded photos or videos), write comments, like and save posts, and send messages to other members. This content is stored in Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage so it can be shown in the community feed.

Community safety: reporting and blocking

You can report any post (selecting a reason) and block any account directly from the post menu. Blocked accounts are hidden from your feed. Reports include the post identifier, reason, your Firebase user identifier, and the report time, and are reviewed so harmful content can be removed.

Diagnostics and crash reporting

To detect and fix crashes, release versions of Noor Pro send anonymous diagnostic information (such as crash stack traces, device model, and operating-system version) to Firebase Crashlytics. This data is used only to improve app stability and is not used to identify you.

Notifications

If enabled, Noor Pro schedules local prayer reminders. Notification permission can be disabled in Android settings.

Third-party services

Noor Pro may communicate with Google Firebase, Google Play Services Location, AlAdhan, AlQuran Cloud, approved Quran recitation hosts, and approved content hosts. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Data sharing and sale

Noor Pro does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to deliver requested app functions, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall Noor Pro. You may sign out at any time. To request deletion of your authenticated account or associated reports, follow the instructions on the account deletion page.

Children

Noor Pro is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the instructions on our support page.
