Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

Noor Pro (package com.noorpro.app) respects your privacy. This policy explains what the Android app and this website process, why, and the choices you have. Contact: noorpro.official@gmail.com.

Information you provide

If you create an account or sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, display name, account identifier, and credentials. Noor Pro never receives your Google password. If you reset your password, we send the reset email through the Resend email service.

Community content

Signed-in users can publish posts, photos, videos and reels, write comments, like and save posts, follow people, and exchange private messages and group messages (text, photos, voice notes and shared location). This content, your public profile (name, username, bio, photo) and related counts are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage, and reel videos are stored and streamed using Amazon Web Services (S3, MediaConvert and CloudFront). Posts, reels, comments and your profile are visible to other users; chats and groups are visible to their participants.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

You can report posts, reels, comments, messages, groups and profiles, and block users. A report contains the content identifier, reason, your user identifier and the time, and is reviewed by us within 24 hours so we can remove harmful content and act against accounts that break our Terms.

Location

With your permission, Noor Pro uses your device location (Google Play Services Location) to calculate prayer times, label your approximate place, and compute Qibla direction. Coordinates are stored on your device and sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time API to retrieve prayer and Hijri information. Location is also shared with other people only if you explicitly send a location in a chat. Maps are provided by Google Maps.

Camera, microphone and media

The camera is used only to take photos or videos you choose to send or post; the microphone is used only to record voice notes. Neither is used in the background.

Noor AI

When you use Noor AI, your question, the selected mode and up to the last 10 messages of that conversation, together with your app-check/sign-in token, are sent to our Firebase Cloud Function, which forwards them to an AI model provider (NVIDIA NIM hosted models) to generate an answer. Our function does not store prompts or answers; it logs only errors. The provider processes the request under its own terms.

Advertising and consent

Noor Pro shows ads using Google AdMob, which may use your Android advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads. Where required (EEA, UK and similar regions), we show Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads and you can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

App activity and local data

Bookmarks, prayer logs, Qaza counts, Noor Points, settings, downloaded reading data, and learning progress are primarily stored locally on your device. If you use cloud backup (Settings), a copy of your settings, your email and your app version is stored in Firestore under your account.

Diagnostics and crash reporting

Release versions send crash reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) to Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability. Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) is used to protect our backend from abuse.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications, Noor Pro schedules prayer reminders locally on your device, an optional ongoing next-prayer notification and optional daily ayah reminders. Exact-alarm access is used only so the adhan sounds on time. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings.

Third-party services

Noor Pro communicates with: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, App Check, Crashlytics, Cloud Functions), Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform, Google Maps and Play Services Location, Credential Manager (Google sign-in), AlAdhan (prayer times), AlQuran Cloud, Quran.com and EveryAyah (Quran text and recitation), GitHub / jsDelivr / Google Drive (library content), LottieFiles (animations), Amazon Web Services (reel storage and streaming), Resend (password-reset email) and NVIDIA (AI). Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Data sharing and sale

Noor Pro does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above to deliver app functions, to comply with law, or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall Noor Pro. You can delete your account and associated data inside the app (Profile > Account > Delete account, or Settings > Support > Delete account) or by following the account deletion page. We then delete your profile, posts, comments, likes, saves, follows, backup and sign-in account; some files and moderation records are purged or retained as described there.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to withdraw consent. Email noorpro.official@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Noor Pro is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16; if you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.

Contact

Privacy questions: noorpro.official@gmail.com or our support page.
