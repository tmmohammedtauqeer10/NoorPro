Features

Everything for your day, nothing you don't need.

Noor Pro is built around the rhythm of a Muslim's day — from Fajr to the last dua before sleep — without noise or clutter.

Quran & recitation

Read all 114 Surahs in a clean Indopak script, resume exactly where you stopped, bookmark ayahs and listen to recitation as you read.

Prayer times & Adhan

Location-aware timings with your chosen calculation authority and Madhab, a live countdown to the next salah, and Adhan notifications that survive a reboot.

Qibla compass

A precise, sensor-based Qibla direction that only uses the compass while the screen is open — so it never drains your battery in the background.

Duas & daily worship

Morning and evening azkar, a digital Tasbih counter, the 99 Names, Hijri calendar, Zakat calculator and a gentle daily tracker with Qaza tools.

Ummah community

Follow people you trust, share reminders, message friends privately and build groups — with every public post passing moderation before it goes live.

Noor AI

Ask respectful questions about Quran, hadith, duas and daily practice. Answers always encourage you to confirm rulings with a qualified scholar.

How it works

Set up once. Then it just stays with you.

Install and open

Download the Android build and open it straight away. There is no sign-up wall — guest mode gives you Quran, prayer times, Qibla and duas immediately.

Set your location

Allow location once so prayer times and Qibla are accurate. Fine-tune the calculation authority, Madhab, Hijri offset and time format in Settings.

Make it yours

Choose your Adhan sound, pick light or dark theme, and sign in only if you want backup, messages and the Ummah community.

Noor AI

A thoughtful companion, not a mufti.

Noor AI helps you understand context, find the right dua and explore the meaning of an ayah in plain language. It is deliberately humble: for anything that touches a ruling, it points you back to a qualified scholar in your own community.

Privacy

Your worship is yours alone.

Noor Pro is designed so that the private parts of your practice stay private — and so that you can leave at any time, taking nothing with you.

Guest mode first

Reading Quran, checking prayer times and finding the Qibla never require an account. Signing in is only for social features and backup.

Moderated community

Public posts are reviewed before they appear, and group media can only be opened by members of that group.

Delete on your terms

You can remove your account and its data whenever you want, straight from the app or from this website.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Is Noor Pro free?
Yes. Noor Pro is free to download and use. A small, clearly marked banner helps cover hosting and development costs, and it never interrupts reading or prayer screens.
Which Android versions are supported?
Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above, with Android 8.0 or newer recommended for reliable Adhan notifications and background scheduling.
Do I need an account?
No. Quran, prayer times, Qibla, duas and Tasbih all work in guest mode. An account is only needed for the Ummah community, private messages, groups and cloud backup of your progress.
Are the prayer times accurate for my city?
Prayer times are calculated from your device location using standard astronomical methods. You can choose your calculation authority, Madhab for Asr, high-latitude rule and a manual Hijri adjustment in Settings so the timings match your local mosque.
Why does the app ask for notification and location permissions?
Location is used to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction on your device. Notifications are used only to remind you at prayer times. Both are optional, and the app clearly degrades if you decline them.
Can I trust Noor AI for religious rulings?
Treat Noor AI as a study aid, not a source of fatwa. It is helpful for context, translations and finding relevant duas, but every answer reminds you to verify rulings with a qualified scholar.
