Support

Use the Feedback option inside Noor Pro Settings to contact support from your configured email app.

Google Sign-In

Confirm that a Google account is added to your Android device, update Google Play Services, and try again. If an error remains, include the exact message shown by Noor Pro.

Prayer times and location

Allow location access, enable device location, then use Settings > Location & API Sync. Confirm your calculation authority, Madhab, Hijri adjustment, and time format.
