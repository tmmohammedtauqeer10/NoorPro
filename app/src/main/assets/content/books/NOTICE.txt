NoorPro - Tasawwuf library: sources and licences

All texts below are public domain (published before 1929) and are reproduced verbatim from the scans listed (only whitespace and line-break hyphenation were normalised; unreadable OCR fragments were dropped).

al-Luma' fi'l-Tasawwuf [en]
  Source/licence: R.A. Nicholson (ed.), The Kitab al-Luma' fi'l-Tasawwuf, E.J.W. Gibb Memorial Series XXII, London 1914 - English Preface and Abstract of Contents only; public domain (published 1914). Internet Archive scan kitaballuma00sarruoft, OCR text.
  Completeness: Arabic text not available as machine-readable public text (the scan OCR contains no Arabic). Only Nicholson's English preface, abstract of contents and glossary are included. OCR paragraphs that were unreadable were omitted.

Kashf al-Mahjub [en]
  Source/licence: The Kashf al-Mahjub, translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, E.J.W. Gibb Memorial Series XVII, Leiden/London 1911; public domain (published 1911). Internet Archive scan 32882019066896-thekashfalmahju, OCR text.
  Completeness: Complete English translation (OCR; footnotes and unreadable fragments may be mixed in or omitted). Persian original not available from an open source.

Kimiya-e-Sa'adat [en]
  Source/licence: The Alchemy of Happiness, by al-Ghazzali, translated from the Hindustani by Claud Field, Wisdom of the East series, John Murray, London 1910; public domain (published 1910). Internet Archive scan alchemyofhappine00algh, OCR text.
  Completeness: An abridgement (8 chapters), not the complete Kimiya.
