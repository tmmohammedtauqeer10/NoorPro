# NoorPro Academy content notes

The original 24-lesson reading course is adapted from the same owner’s Deenflow Tasawwuf content:
https://github.com/tmmohammedtauqeer10/Deenflow/blob/main/tasawwuf_api.json

It remains an introductory editorial draft and does not claim instructor-recorded lectures or scholarly accreditation.

External course descriptions in catalog.json are short original summaries. Third-party lectures and textbooks are not bundled. Provider links and access evidence are documented in docs/ACADEMY_COURSES.md. No affiliation or redistribution license is implied.
